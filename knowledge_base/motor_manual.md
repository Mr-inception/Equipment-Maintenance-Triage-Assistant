title: Three-Phase Induction Motor Maintenance Manual

## [MOT-1.1] Winding overheating
Winding temperature is normally below 105 degC. Warning above 130 degC. Critical above 150 degC: stop the motor. Common causes: sustained overload, blocked ventilation or cooling fan damage, low supply voltage, phase imbalance, too many starts per hour, bearing friction. Inspection: check cooling airflow and fan cover, measure supply voltage on all phases, compare running current against the nameplate, review recent start frequency.

## [MOT-1.2] Motor will not start or trips on start
Common causes: tripped thermal overload, blown fuse, single phasing, mechanically seized load, failed contactor, low supply voltage. Inspection: confirm supply on all three phases, check overload relay and fuses, try to turn the shaft by hand with power locked out, inspect contactor contacts.

## [MOT-1.3] Abnormal vibration or noise
Alert above 7.1 mm/s RMS. Common causes: bearing wear, rotor imbalance, loose mounting bolts, coupling misalignment, broken rotor bar (pulsing noise and current). Inspection: check mounting bolts, check coupling alignment, listen for bearing noise, look for pulsing current on the ammeter.

## [MOT-1.4] High current or current imbalance
Running current more than 10 percent above nameplate is abnormal. Imbalance of more than 5 percent between phases is abnormal. Common causes: overload, supply voltage imbalance, shorted turns, loose terminal connections. Inspection: measure all three phase currents and voltages, check terminal tightness, inspect the driven load for binding.

## [MOT-1.5] Motor bearing failure signs
Bearing temperature warning above 85 degC, critical above 95 degC. Signs: grinding or squealing noise, discoloured or dry grease, rising vibration. Common causes: lack of grease, over-greasing, shaft currents, misalignment. Inspection: check grease condition and schedule, listen with a stethoscope, check alignment.

## [MOT-2.1] Safe inspection and lockout
Before any hands-on inspection, isolate and lock out the supply and verify zero voltage. Allow the motor to cool and the rotor to stop. Restart after maintenance requires technician sign-off. This system never controls equipment remotely.
