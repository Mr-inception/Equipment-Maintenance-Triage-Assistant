title: Rotary Screw Air Compressor Maintenance Manual

## [COMP-1.1] High discharge temperature
Normal air-end discharge temperature is 70 to 100 degC. Warning above 110 degC. Critical above 120 degC: the unit should trip. Common causes: blocked cooler fins, low oil level, failed cooling fan, clogged oil filter, high ambient temperature, worn oil separator element. Inspection: check oil level and condition, clean cooler fins, confirm the cooling fan runs, check ambient ventilation, check oil filter differential.

## [COMP-1.2] Low discharge pressure or long loading time
Rated working pressure is 7 to 8 bar. Pressure below 6 bar while loaded is abnormal. Common causes: air leaks in downstream piping, clogged intake filter, stuck unloader or inlet valve, slipping drive belt, excessive demand. Inspection: check the intake filter, listen for leaks, inspect the inlet valve, check belt tension.

## [COMP-1.3] Oil carryover into the air line
Oil in downstream air or in the receiver drain indicates carryover. Common causes: saturated oil separator element, oil level too high, blocked scavenge line, faulty minimum pressure valve. Inspection: check oil level against the sight glass, check separator element differential pressure, inspect the scavenge line.

## [COMP-1.4] Abnormal vibration or noise
Alert above 7.1 mm/s RMS. Common causes: loose mounting bolts, worn coupling, bearing wear, failed internal valves. Inspection: check mounting and anti-vibration pads, inspect the coupling, listen for bearing noise, check recent maintenance records.

## [COMP-1.5] Short cycling (frequent start and stop)
Common causes: pressure switch differential set too narrow, large air leak, undersized receiver, condensate drain stuck open. Inspection: check pressure switch settings, check the drain valve, check for leaks.

## [COMP-2.1] Safe inspection and lockout
Before any hands-on inspection, lock out power, fully depressurise the system and let hot parts cool. Never open a pressurised line. Restart after maintenance requires technician sign-off. This system never controls equipment remotely.
